a small thing, written carefully

For the girl who makes every moment feel like it matters.

Brynlee, this is a letter about everything I’ve been feeling but never quite knew how to put into words, because some things deserve to be said in a way you can hold onto forever.

01 / the letter

I have learned that the smallest moments can mean the most.

It is in the message you send when a thought is still warm. In the way a call can begin with, “I have nothing to say,” and somehow leave a whole evening feeling held. It is in the hours spent talking about everything and nothing, in falling asleep on the phone, and in the nights spent playing games together where it hardly matters what we’re doing, as long as you’re there.

I think there is something special about the moments that do not need to be planned. The conversations that stretch far past when we meant to go to sleep, the games that somehow turn into hours, and the calls where neither of us really has anything left to say, but neither of us wants to be the first to hang up. Somehow, even the quiet moments feel like something worth keeping.

But you make the ordinary feel like something I will remember. It is not always about doing something exciting or having something important to say. Sometimes it is just hearing your voice, laughing over something stupid, or knowing that somewhere in the middle of an ordinary day, I get to share a little piece of it with you. And I think that is what I love most—not that every moment is extraordinary, but that somehow, with you, even the simplest ones feel like they are.

02 / the little things

The moments I keep coming back to are the simplest.

I love how easy it is to tell you everything: the thoughts that arrive out of nowhere, the jokes that would make no sense to anyone else, and the quiet that never needs to be filled. You make the ordinary feel warm, familiar, and worth remembering.

the part I have been carrying around

Brynlee, will you be my girlfriend?

No perfect answer is required. Just the honest one that feels like yours.

Then it is a yes from me, too.

Thank you for choosing this with me, Brynlee. I cannot promise every day will be easy, but I can promise to keep showing up with an open heart and a message waiting.
